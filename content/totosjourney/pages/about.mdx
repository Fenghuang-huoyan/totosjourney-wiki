This is a short English wiki for **Toto's Journey**, Steam AppID **3724300**, built as a static site for **totosjourney.wiki**.

## What the pages will and will not say

The [guide](/guide), [walkthrough](/walkthrough), [puzzles](/puzzles), and [collectibles](/collectibles) pages quote dated Steam text. A place name, a puzzle rule, or an item appears only when the store page or a Hanuman Games post on Steam used it. There is no codes page because those sources do not publish a redeem program. The October 2026 demo post says the demo needs no key.

A sentence about the Oz books stays inside the studio's own wording. The 5 September 2025 announcement calls the game an independent creation inspired by L. Frank Baum's public-domain *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz* (1900), and says it is not affiliated with trademark holders. This site does not claim any other relationship to the books or the films.

<Callout kind="note" title="What “checked” means">
“Checked 12 Nov 2026” means the Steam API, the English store page, the demo listing, and the linked news posts were read that day. It does not mean someone finished the launch build. The full game was still marked coming soon, with the store date 18 November 2026.
</Callout>

## Images

The library hero, capsules, the store header, and screenshots are official Steam store assets for AppID 3724300. They were downloaded from the Steam CDN and are served from this site so pages do not hotlink Steam. The file list and source URLs are in [ATTRIBUTION.txt](/images/official/steam/ATTRIBUTION.txt). The favicon is original. It is not a Hanuman Games or Valve logo, and it is not the key art from the store capsule.

## Privacy and ads

The site is static. It has no accounts and does not ask for your Steam login.

GA4 `G-78M48W325M` is set in `analytics.gaId`. The base layout loads that tag on content pages.

The Adsterra Native Banner slot named `afterQuickAnswer` is enabled for totosjourney.wiki. Zone id `fcf6a95cfcc7af2cfde360dcf6850bb4` loads from `https://bauval.org/21/fcf6a95cfcc7af2cfde360dcf6850bb4` on the homepage and guide pages. No advertising script from another game is included. Adult and sensitive categories stay off. The 404 page does not render a slot.

The same privacy note is in the footer of every page. The legal wording is also on [Privacy](/privacy) and [Disclaimer](/disclaimer).

## What is still unpublished

- A numeric Steam price. The 12 November appdetails payload had an empty package list, and `is_free` was false.
- A puzzle solution, a collectible count, and a chapter order.
- Whether the 18 November build keeps the diary names for the Star Pebble, the Bronze Seed, and the Turban of Winds.
- Whether the Steam Deck plan in the 30 September 2026 post is a store listing. The platforms field that day in November listed Windows only.
