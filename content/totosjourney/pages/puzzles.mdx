This is the puzzle-solutions page for **Toto's Journey**. The solutions that exist in official text are the general rules below. A numbered answer for a named room does not, in the sources checked on 12 November 2026. This page does not invent one. The full game was still coming soon.

Items that the diaries tie to movement and puzzles are listed again, with their story text, on [Collectibles](/collectibles).

## What the store page says

The about text says you face puzzles that test logic and creativity. The three actions it names are:

- Open new paths.
- Restore broken mechanisms.
- Interact with light and shadows.

It says each solved challenge makes Toto stronger and unlocks magical abilities. It does not show a mechanism, a light source, a shadow, or the ability you unlock. The 30 September 2026 "what to expect" post says you progress by exploring, interacting with the environment, and finding ways past obstacles. That is the same level of detail.

## The one instruction printed on a screenshot

One of the eleven store screenshots is a tutorial card. The card's title and body, read from the full-size store image, are:

> TELEKINES: OBSTACLES
>
> When a pink paw appears in front of a large object, press E / Circle to lift it with telekinesis and release the passage.

The inset on that card shows the dog in a purple and gold outfit and hat, under a boulder. The card does not name the place, the boulder, or the outfit. "Circle" matches the store's DualSense and DualShock categories, and "E" is a keyboard key. The card is a rule for a prompt. It is not a map of every obstacle.

A 25 August 2026 diary, written before that screenshot was checked for this page, describes two related interactions without using the word telekinesis:

- Small objects that Toto can levitate. The post says picking up, moving, and throwing them was made clearer, with fewer extra actions.
- Heavy obstacles he cannot pick up or drag. The post says some of them can be moved with a properly timed push.

This page does not merge the screenshot's "lift with telekinesis" and the diary's "timed push" into one button. They may be the same idea in two posts, or two different interactions. The sources do not say.

The same August post says a careless jump can still send Toto over an edge, and that the result is trying again. It does not name the edge.

## Crystals and the bark

The 12 March 2026 diary says magic crystals are Toto's main way of interacting with the Magical Land. It says they are in caves, among fields, and deep in forests. The rule it prints:

- A loud bark makes large crystals resonate and emit waves of magic.
- The same bark shatters small crystals into fragments.
- The fragments are a currency. They can be spent on repairing destroyed bridges, restoring locals' homes, and unlocking new abilities.

The post does not say how many shards a repair costs, which bridge, which home, or which ability. It does not say the bark is the only way to break a crystal. Those costs are not on this page because they were not in the post.

## Digging

The 17 June 2026 diary says some secrets are under loose earth, and that the team places specific spots where Toto can dig. It says what is underneath might be a new path into the underground, an unusual item, or something else, and it tells the reader that part is for them to find out. A dig spot is a puzzle type only in the sense that you have to notice the loose earth. The diary does not mark those spots on a map.

The 30 September 2026 post says the same thing more briefly: some secrets and discoveries are hidden underground.

## Items that change a puzzle, without a location

Three diaries describe an item and an effect. None of them says where the item is, or which puzzle requires it. The effects, as those posts word them:

- **Star Pebble** (4 August 2026). In kind hands it shares its power and temporarily weakens the pull of gravity. The post says Toto can then open new paths, reach inaccessible areas, and solve unusual puzzles. It also says playtest players may already know what the magic does. It does not describe the playtest puzzle.
- **Bronze Seed** (12 August 2026). Kept away from the soil, it lets Toto control plant roots and shoots, so he can move through dense vegetation, clear obstacles, and pass thorn bushes. The post's warning: do not plant it near metal deposits. The rest of that diary is a story about a herbalist and a Bronze Forest. It is not a puzzle layout.
- **Turban of Winds** (9 September 2026). It lets Toto run faster and make quicker jumps, using the wind to clear obstacles and reach places that were out of reach. The post hopes this helps him get closer to the hurricane that carried Dorothy away. It does not show that scene.

## Hints the diaries mention

The 28 July 2026 diary introduces Sage Toads in the swamps of the Blue Country. It says that if Toto asks politely, they will offer a helpful hint or some swampy wisdom, and that their most important conversations happen on Wednesdays. The post does not print a hint. Asking a toad is not a solution this page can copy.

The 5 March 2026 diary says mountain Munchkins will help Toto on his way and equip him if he shows courage and responsiveness. It does not list the tasks or the gear.

<Callout kind="note" title="Not a solution">
A pink-paw prompt, a loose patch of earth, a crystal, a toad, and three named items are the puzzle facts these sources printed. A combination order, a skip, and a "just do this" for a specific room were not. If you are in the demo and a prompt on screen disagrees with a diary from the spring, follow the prompt on screen.
</Callout>
