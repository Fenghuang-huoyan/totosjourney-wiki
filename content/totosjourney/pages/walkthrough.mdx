There is no step-by-step walkthrough of **Toto's Journey** yet, because the full game was still marked coming soon on 12 November 2026. This page collects the place names and journey sentences that Steam and Hanuman Games have actually published. It does not turn them into a route, and it does not number rooms.

How the game is described, including demo facts and the PC spec, is on the [guide](/guide). Puzzle types are on [Puzzles](/puzzles).

## The only story order the store gives

The store's about text is one journey, not a chapter list:

1. A mysterious cyclone sweeps Toto into a magical world and separates him from Dorothy.
2. He looks for a way home, meets allies, and uncovers secrets.
3. Along the way he explores distinct places and solves environmental puzzles. The store says he grows stronger and unlocks magical abilities that help him keep searching for Dorothy.

That is the whole sequence the store page prints. It does not say which place comes first after the storm, or how the search ends.

The 15 October 2026 demo post uses different storm wording and the same goal: Toto is separated from Dorothy by a hurricane, explores the Magical Land, solves its puzzles, and tries to find his way back to his owner. The post calls the demo a small part of that journey. It does not say which part.

## Place names on the store page

One sentence in the about text names two locations:

- Dense forests hiding the Lion.
- Mechanical ruins once inhabited by the Tin Woodman.

The store says each area has its own challenges and mixes exploration with environmental storytelling. It does not say the Lion is a character you meet, a boss, or only a place name. It does not say the Tin Woodman is present. It says the ruins were once inhabited by him. This page does not add a meeting, a fight, or a puzzle inside either place.

No other proper place name appears in the store's about text. "Enchanted lands" and "a magical world" are the other phrases.

## Place names in the diaries

These are the names the developer posts use. The date is the post, not a promise that the 18 November build still uses the name.

| Name in the post | What that post says | Post |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Magic Land, Magical Land | Both spellings appear. The 30 September 2026 post uses an unfamiliar world and a magical world. The 5 March 2026 diary says the Magic Land. | Several, from 2026 |
| Blue Country, Four Countries | Munchkins are called inhabitants of the Blue Country. Sage Toads are said to know things "like few in all Four Countries." | 5 Mar 2026 and 28 Jul 2026 |
| Mountain settlement of Munchkins | The 5 March 2026 diary says one stage of the journey begins in a mountain settlement at the foot of the cliffs, where Munchkins mine ore, harvest timber, and send it downriver. It says the tornado disrupted that work and Toto helps with the aftermath. It also says most Munchkins live in peaceful valleys and farm, and that Toto meets the mountain settlement rather than the valley dwellers. | 5 Mar 2026 |
| Caves and mines | The same diary says the path also goes through underground caves and mines where Munchkins extract emeralds and metal, with galleries and waterfalls. A 7 April 2026 diary says cave systems matter and Toto will be underground more than once. | 5 Mar 2026 and 7 Apr 2026 |
| Forests, meadows, a swamp | A 14 May 2026 diary says the main biomes will feature deep forests and endless meadows, and shows a swamp Toto will try not to fall into. | 14 May 2026 |
| Swamps on the way to caves | A 9 June 2026 diary shares concept art for swamps Toto has to cross to reach the caves. The post says the images are concepts, and that final locations would be compared later. | 9 Jun 2026 |
| Opening lands | A 5 September 2025 art post describes the start of the journey as soft flora, sunlit architecture, a windmill, a stone arch, and cozy cottages, with grander buildings deeper in. That post is about art direction. | 5 Sep 2025 |

The Lion's forests and the Tin Woodman's ruins are store sentences from the current page. They are not in the diary table above, and the diaries do not say where those two places sit relative to the Blue Country. This page does not stitch them into one map.

<Callout kind="warn" title="A diary is not a chapter">
"One of the stages" is the 5 March 2026 diary's own phrase for the mountain Munchkins. It is not "Chapter 1" of a published walkthrough. The swamp-to-caves line is one sentence about concept art. The windmill is from an art-direction post a year before the store date. None of these posts lists exits, items in a room, or a fail state.
</Callout>

No screenshot caption on the store page names these scenes. An on-screen E prompt and a tutorial card are UI, not a quest log. This page does not treat a sparkle, a pot, or a rodent as a collectible.

## The demo, without a path

The public demo is the only build a store post tells you to download without a request. Facts, dates, and the empty description fields are on the [guide](/guide). For a walkthrough, the useful limit is this: the 15 October 2026 post says you explore the Magical Land, solve its puzzles, and look for the way back to Dorothy, and that the demo is a small part of the journey. It stops there.

Playtest posts from November 2025 and from July and August 2026 describe older request-access builds. Those posts say the tests ended. They are not a current route, and the 8 July 2026 note about "a new level" does not name the level.
