**Toto's Journey** has no public collectible list with totals, missable flags, or locations. This page is the list of things Hanuman Games has named in Steam posts, plus the categories those posts mention without naming a single object. A sack of sparks in a screenshot is not added here, because the store page never says what that sack is.

How those items affect puzzles is on [Puzzles](/puzzles). The release and the demo are on the [guide](/guide).

## What the studio said it was adding

The 30 September 2026 post says a full playthrough was expected to take about 5–6 hours if you follow the main path, and longer if you explore, look for secrets, collect items, and dig. It says collectibles and hidden secrets are there to reward that looking. It does not name one collectible.

The 28 October 2026 release announcement says you discover magical items and find new ways to explore, and that Toto's nose can sniff out secrets and hidden paths. It does not name an item either. The named items are all from earlier diaries.

## Crystal shards

The 12 March 2026 diary is the only post that describes a currency.

Magic crystals are scattered through caves, fields, and forests. A loud bark resonates the large ones and shatters the small ones. The shards from small clusters, or from stones you break, are what Toto spends. The diary says the shards help repair destroyed bridges, restore locals' homes, and unlock new abilities that let him go deeper into the Magical Land.

No price, no shard cap, and no list of unlocks was in that post. This page does not invent a shop table.

## Named magical items

The 4 August 2026 diary introduces "Magical Items" as discoveries that grant a power and open exploration and puzzle-solving. It says more artifacts would be revealed in later diaries. Three items have been named in the posts checked. Two of the diaries are both numbered 013. They are different posts. This table uses the date and the title.

| Item | What the post says it does | What the post does not say | Post |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Star Pebble | A shard of an enchanted star. In kind hands it calms and temporarily weakens gravity, so Toto can open paths, reach places, and solve unusual puzzles. Playtest players "may already have an idea" of the magic. | Where it is. What the playtest puzzle was. Whether the final game still includes it. | Magical Items, 4 Aug 2026 |
| Bronze Seed | Kept off the soil, it lets Toto control roots and shoots, clear vegetation, and pass thorn bushes. The warning in the post: do not plant it near metal deposits. The story around it mentions a Bronze Bud that stopped a Bronze Forest. | Where Toto finds the Seed. Whether the Bud is a separate item you can hold. | The Bronze Seed, 12 Aug 2026 |
| Turban of Winds | Also called the Wind Sash in the same post. It lets Toto run faster and jump more quickly, and use the wind to pass obstacles and reach places that were inaccessible. | Where it is. Whether the sash and the turban are two objects or two names. | Turban of Winds, 9 Sep 2026 |

The Star Pebble diary says the items "will" accompany Toto. The Turban diary says artifacts Toto "may" discover. This page keeps both verbs. It does not promote a "may" into a guaranteed pickup on 18 November.

The Bronze Bud is part of the Seed's backstory in that diary: a herbalist plucked it and the Bronze Forest rusted. The post does not say Toto collects the Bud.

<Callout kind="note" title="Story text is not a drop table">
The Wind Sash "tends to disappear whenever it gets bored" is a line in the Turban diary. It is not a rule for despawning loot. The Bronze Forest is the diary's account of what the Seed once did to a mountain. It is not a zone this page can point to on a map.
</Callout>

## Categories with no names

These phrases appear in official posts. No proper name is attached.

- **Keepsake trinkets.** The 24 March 2026 diary says that while traveling through levels, Toto will discover secrets, keepsake trinkets, hidden characters, puzzles, and ways to look stylish. It does not name a trinket or a cosmetic.
- **Special outfits.** The 8 July 2026 playtest announcement says that build's reworked abilities come from special outfits that give Toto unique powers. It does not name an outfit. One store screenshot's tutorial inset shows the dog in a purple and gold outfit and hat. The screenshot does not label the clothes, so this page does not give them a name.
- **Dig finds.** The 17 June 2026 diary says a loose spot might hide a pathway, an unusual item, or something else, and refuses to say which. The 30 September post adds that players can dig up anything that looks suspicious. Neither post lists a dug-up item.
- **Something special in the caves.** The 5 March 2026 diary says Toto might find something special in the Munchkin mines if he looks carefully. It does not say what.

## What would be an invention

A total such as "30 bones" or "100 percent." A map pin. A missable flag. A statement that the purple outfit is required, optional, or in the demo. A claim that crystal shards are the only collectible. None of those sentences is in the sources above.

Screenshots of sparks, cooking pots, and camps are on the [walkthrough](/walkthrough) page as pictures. They stay pictures until a post says what the object is.
