## What Steam says the game is

The short description on the store:

> Toto’s Journey is a cozy adventure inspired by the world of The Wizard of Oz. Play as Toto, a brave dog lost in a magical world after a mysterious cyclone. Explore enchanted lands, meet unusual creatures, and solve environmental puzzles as you search for a way back home.

The longer text says you play as Toto, a brave and loyal dog swept into a magical world and separated from his owner Dorothy. He looks for a way back home, meets new allies, and uncovers secrets. Locations in that paragraph run from dense forests hiding the Lion to mechanical ruins once inhabited by the Tin Woodman. Puzzles ask you to open paths, restore broken mechanisms, and use light and shadows. The store says each solved challenge makes Toto stronger and unlocks new magical abilities. It calls the game a heartfelt adventure about friendship, discovery, and the desire to return home.

A 28 October 2026 studio post announces the same date: Toto's Journey will be released on 18 November. A 30 September 2026 post had only said Q4 2026 and that the exact date would be announced separately. The Steam date is the current listing.

## Oz, and what the studio says it is not

The store says the game is inspired by the world of The Wizard of Oz. The 30 September 2026 post is more specific. It names L. Frank Baum's *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz*, says you will find a few small references to the original story, and says Toto's Journey is the studio's own interpretation rather than a direct adaptation of the book.

The first developer diary, posted 5 September 2025, uses the same line and adds a footnote: the game is an independent creation inspired by the public-domain book of 1900, and it is not affiliated with trademark holders. Original characters and content are called the property of Hanuman Games.

This wiki repeats those sentences. It does not claim that Hanuman Games, this site, or the game is affiliated with any Oz film or later book.

The store and the diaries do not use one word for the storm. The store says cyclone. The October demo posts say hurricane. The 5 March 2026 diary says tornado. This page keeps the word each source used.

## What you do as Toto

The 28 October 2026 announcement lists five things the game offers: explore a magical world inspired by The Wizard of Oz, use Toto's nose to sniff out secrets and hidden paths, meet unusual inhabitants and help them, solve puzzles and uncover mysteries, and discover magical items that open new ways to explore.

The 30 September 2026 post expands the same ideas and adds a length estimate. The studio said it expected a full playthrough of about 5–6 hours, depending on whether you follow the main path or explore, collect, and dig. It also said collectibles and hidden secrets are there for players who look around. That figure is the studio's expectation on that date. It is not a time measured on the 18 November build, which was not out when this page was written.

The same post names the engine: Unreal Engine 5.

Actions the posts actually describe:

- **Follow a scent.** The 30 September post says Toto can pick up scents, find hidden trails and clues, and notice things that are not obvious. A 24 March 2026 diary calls him a tracker who can catch the wind with his nose. It also says the visual effect for that sniff was still in test.
- **Dig.** The 30 September post says some secrets are underground. A 17 June 2026 diary says the team places spots where the earth is loose enough to dig, and that a dig might hide a path, an item, or something else. It does not say which.
- **Solve environmental puzzles.** The store's examples are new paths, broken mechanisms, and light and shadows. The diaries add crystals, heavy objects, and small objects you can move. Those mechanics are quoted on the [puzzles](/puzzles) page. None of the posts is a solution.
- **Gain abilities from items.** The store says Toto unlocks magical abilities. Diaries name a Star Pebble, a Bronze Seed, and a Turban of Winds. Effects and the limits of those posts are on [Collectibles](/collectibles).
- **Meet inhabitants.** The store says unusual creatures and new allies. Diaries name Munchkins of the Blue Country and Sage Toads. They do not publish a character list or a dialogue tree.

The 5 September 2025 announcement also says the game blends storytelling, logic-based puzzles, and light combat. Later posts describe barking, digging, jumping, and moving objects. They do not publish a combat system, a weapon, or damage numbers. A 26 February 2026 diary says Toto's animations were recorded with motion capture and lists barking, biting, and jumping as his toolkit. It does not name the performer.
