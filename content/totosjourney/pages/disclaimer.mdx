totosjourney.wiki is an unofficial fan-made guide for **Toto's Journey**. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Hanuman Games, Creata Games, Valve, or Steam**. It is not affiliated with the books or films of Oz. All game content, names, artwork, and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Hanuman Games' own first Steam diary, posted 5 September 2025, calls Toto's Journey an independent creation inspired by the public-domain work *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz* by L. Frank Baum (1900), and says that game is not affiliated with trademark holders. This wiki is a separate fan site. It is not the developer, and it does not speak for the developer.

This site does not sell Toto's Journey, process purchases, redeem codes, or recover accounts. It is not responsible for purchase decisions, or for a pre-release post that the 18 November 2026 build later changes. On 12 November 2026 the Steam store still marked the full game coming soon.

Nothing here is account support. The Steam store page lists the email community@hanumangames.com and does not list a support URL. The website field on that page is https://totosjourney.com/. That mailbox and that site are not this wiki's. Mail sent to the studio does not reach the people who edit these pages.

The guide quotes dated official text. Where the store has not named a puzzle answer, a collectible count, a price, or a code, the page says so instead of filling the gap. Steam store artwork is used to illustrate those pages and is credited in the attribution file next to the images. The site's favicon is original and is not a Hanuman Games, Creata Games, or Valve logo.

Steam posts and screenshots can change without notice. A "checked" date is the day the source was read, not a promise that the sentence is still true in the current client.

The privacy note for this static site is on [Privacy](/privacy). A shorter copy of this disclaimer is in the footer of every page.
